🪄 Hidden Typing Tricks Secret Features

Generate Random Text

Type: =rand() and press Enter.

Microsoft Word automatically creates tutorial text.

Lorem Ipsum Generator

Type: =lorem() then press Enter.

Perfect for layouts, brochures and mockups.

Create Horizontal Lines

Type: ---, ===, *** then press Enter.

Auto Date Insert

Press: Alt + Shift + D

Automatically inserts current date.

Auto Time Insert

Press: Alt + Shift + T

Automatically inserts current time.

Symbols Auto Convert

  • (c) → ©
  • (r) → ®
  • (tm) → ™
  • --> → →
🔳 QR Code Creation Guide Advanced

Text QR Code

Insert → Get Add-ins → Search QR4Office → Add → Enter text → Generate QR

Useful for notes, attendance and classroom systems.

Website QR

Paste website URL like:

https://yourwebsite.com

WiFi QR

Format:

WIFI:T:WPA;S:OfficeWiFi;P:12345678;;

Phone Call QR

Example:

tel:+919999999999

Email QR

Example:

mailto:demo@gmail.com

WhatsApp QR

Example:

https://wa.me/919999999999

UPI Payment QR

Example:

upi://pay?pa=demo@upi&pn=Demo

Location QR

Share Google Maps locations instantly through QR scanning.

QR codes can be used for:

• Digital payments • Attendance systems • WiFi sharing • Event registration • Business cards • Menus • Product catalogs • Certificates • Marketing campaigns • Smart classrooms

🎙 Voice Typing Speech to Text

  • Allow microphone permission
  • Start speaking naturally

Voice Commands
