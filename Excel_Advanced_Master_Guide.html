🧮 Excel Formulas & Functions Master Guide Advanced Formulas

SUM Formula

Add numbers automatically.

=SUM(A1:A10)
Used for totals, invoices, reports and dashboards.

AVERAGE Formula

Calculate average values.

=AVERAGE(A1:A10)

MAX & MIN

Find highest and lowest values.

=MAX(A1:A10) =MIN(A1:A10)

COUNT Formula

Count numeric entries.

=COUNT(A1:A100)
🧠 Logical Formulas Decision Making

IF Formula

Perform logical testing.

=IF(A1>=50,"PASS","FAIL")
Useful for marksheets and approval systems.

IFS Formula

Multiple conditions handling.

=IFS(A1>=90,"A+",A1>=75,"A",A1>=50,"B")

AND Formula

=AND(A1>50,B1="YES")

Checks if all conditions are true.

OR Formula

=OR(A1>50,B1="YES")

Checks if any condition is true.

NOT Formula

=NOT(A1>50)

Reverse logical condition.

🔍 Lookup Formulas Data Search

VLOOKUP

Search vertically in tables.

=VLOOKUP(A2,$F$2:$H$100,2,FALSE)
Used in billing, inventory and CRM systems.

HLOOKUP

=HLOOKUP(A1,B1:H5,2,FALSE)

Search horizontally across rows.

XLOOKUP

=XLOOKUP(A2,F2:F100,G2:G100)

Modern replacement of VLOOKUP.

INDEX MATCH

=INDEX(B2:B10,MATCH(E2,A2:A10,0))

Advanced flexible lookup system.

🔤 Text Formulas Text Processing

LEFT Formula

=LEFT(A1,5)

Extract left-side characters.

RIGHT Formula

=RIGHT(A1,4)

Extract right-side text.

MID Formula

=MID(A1,3,5)

Extract middle text.

LEN Formula

=LEN(A1)

Count total characters.

CONCAT Formula

=CONCAT(A1,\" \",B1)

Combine multiple text values.

TEXTJOIN Formula

=TEXTJOIN(\",\",TRUE,A1:A10)

Merge text with separator.
